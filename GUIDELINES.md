# Basketball Manager — Project Guidelines

A EuroLeague basketball **management simulation game** for the web. You play as
both **head coach and general manager** of a real EuroLeague club, building your
roster, setting tactics, and competing across multiple seasons against AI and
other human managers.

This document is the source of truth for what we're building. It was assembled
from a guided Q&A and should be kept up to date as decisions evolve.

---

## 1. Vision

- **Type:** Management simulation game (not a real-world admin tool).
- **Tone:** Realistic & serious — real depth, sim-driven, for basketball fans.
- **Your role:** Both coach *and* GM (full control of team + front office).
- **Fantasy:** Take a real EuroLeague club and lead it to glory over multiple
  seasons — draft-free roster building, deep tactics, and a living league.
- **Platform:** Web app (browser, works everywhere).
- **Business model:** Free. (Door left open for cosmetics/premium later.)
- **Language:** English (v1).

## 2. Content & Realism

- **League:** EuroLeague — full **18 teams** at launch with real clubs.
- **Players:** Real EuroLeague players. Ratings/attributes are **generated by us**
  from public knowledge (no scraping dependency in v1).
- **Player depth:** **Detailed attributes** (shooting, passing, rebounding,
  defense, speed, IQ, stamina, etc.) — not just a single overall rating.
- **Branding:** EuroLeague-inspired visual identity (orange/black palette,
  broadcast/sports-app feel).
- **Images:** Goal is real team logos and player photos. Because of image
  rights, **v1 ships with generated placeholder crests/avatars**, with a clean
  path to swap in real assets later.

> ⚠️ **Rights note:** Real EuroLeague names, logos, and photos carry IP/licensing
> considerations. We use real *names* for realism and *placeholder* imagery to
> start; revisit licensing before any public/commercial release.

## 3. Core Features (v1 must-haves)

**Team**
- Roster management (add/edit players, positions, ratings, contracts)
- Lineups & depth chart (starters, rotations, minutes)
- Player stats tracking (per-game + season: pts, reb, ast, efficiency, …)
- Player development (training, progression, aging, potential)

**Front office (GM)**
- Trades & signings (trade players, sign free agents)
- Finances & salary cap (budgets, contracts, cap rules)
- Injuries & morale (injuries affect availability; morale/chemistry affect play)
- *(Draft is intentionally out of scope for v1.)*

**League / season**
- Schedule & calendar (full EuroLeague fixture list)
- Standings table (records, rankings)
- Playoffs (bracket to crown a champion)
- Awards & records (MVP, top scorer, all-star teams, hall of fame)

## 4. Match & Season Simulation

- **Engine:** Stat-based math sim — outcomes computed from player attributes +
  tactics, producing a full box score.
- **In-game control:** **Adjust during the game** — the match sims out and can be
  paused at key moments for subs/tactical tweaks.
- **Tactics depth:** **Deep** — set plays, matchups, pressing, rotations, minute
  limits, pace, offensive/defensive focus.
- **Time flow:** Game-by-game for the managed club (advance a fixture at a time),
  with the rest of the league simulating around it. *(Refine during prototyping.)*
- **AI:** A single, well-tuned balanced AI difficulty for v1.

## 5. Career

- **Multi-season career:** Manage across seasons — players age, develop, and
  retire; rosters and the league evolve year over year.

## 6. Accounts & Multiplayer

- **Login:** **Required** (accounts for saves + multiplayer).
- **Multiplayer (target):** Support all three modes over time —
  1. **Shared league** — each human manages one club; the rest are AI.
  2. **Private leagues** — create/join with friends via invite code.
  3. **Head-to-head** — challenge another user's team; ladder/ranking.
- **Match timing:** **Asynchronous** — managers set tactics anytime; matches sim
  automatically when both are ready or a deadline hits. No need to be online
  together.

## 7. Tech Direction

Chosen for a multiplayer web game with accounts, persistence, and async sim:

- **Frontend:** React (component-driven UI, good for data-heavy screens).
- **Backend / data / auth:** Supabase (Postgres database, authentication,
  row-level security, realtime) — fits required login + multiplayer + cloud saves.
- **Sim engine:** Deterministic, seedable JS/TS module (shared logic, testable),
  runnable client-side for solo and server-side for authoritative multiplayer.
- **Persistence:** Cloud (Supabase Postgres) so saves and leagues sync across
  devices and support async multiplayer.

*(Stack was "no preference" — this is the recommended default and can change.)*

## 8. Build Sequence

**Core loop first, multiplayer next.** Get to something playable fast, then layer
in online leagues.

### Milestone 1 — Playable single-player prototype (the MVP) ✅ built (mock data)
The end-to-end loop working:
1. ~~Sign up / log in~~ → **mock login** (username only; real auth in M3). ✅
2. Pick a EuroLeague club (all 18 teams). ✅
3. View/manage roster and set a lineup + tactics. ✅
4. Sim a game (stat-based) and view the box score. ✅
5. See standings update; advance to the next fixture. ✅

Implemented with React + Vite over a **mock data layer** (`src/data`,
`src/engine`, `src/state`) that persists to browser localStorage. The store API
is shaped to be swapped for Supabase with minimal churn.

### Milestone 2 — Depth
Detailed attributes & development, deep tactics, injuries/morale, finances &
salary cap, trades & signings, full schedule + playoffs + awards, multi-season
career rollover.

### Milestone 3 — Multiplayer
Shared leagues, private leagues (invite codes), head-to-head, asynchronous match
resolution, ladders/rankings.

### Later
Real logos/photos (licensing permitting), Spanish localization, more difficulty
levels, cosmetics/premium.

## 9. Data Model (initial sketch)

To be refined in Milestone 1. Core entities:

- **User** — auth account, owns saves/league memberships.
- **League / Season** — a world instance; fixtures, standings, awards.
- **Team** — club identity, colors, finances, salary cap, roster.
- **Player** — bio + detailed attributes, contract, form, morale, injury status.
- **Game (Fixture)** — teams, date, tactics snapshot, result, box score.
- **PlayerGameStat / PlayerSeasonStat** — accumulated performance.
- **Transaction** — trades, signings, contract changes.

## 10. Open Questions / To Revisit

- Exact salary-cap rules (EuroLeague has no hard cap like the NBA — model a
  budget/luxury system instead).
- Playoff format (current EuroLeague: top-8 play-in/quarterfinals → Final Four).
- How aggressively AI teams trade/develop.
- Licensing path for real logos/photos before public launch.
